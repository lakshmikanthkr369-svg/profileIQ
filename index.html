What is an ATS resume checker?

An ATS (Applicant Tracking System) resume checker compares your resume with a job description the way hiring software does. ProfileIQ gives you a 0–100 match score and shows which important keywords and skills are missing.

Is ProfileIQ free?

Yes. The free plan includes 3 resume scans every month with an ATS score and keyword analysis. Pro costs ₹199 per month and adds unlimited scans, the AI resume rewrite and PDF/Word downloads.

Does ProfileIQ work for freshers and students?

Yes. Freshers can check their resume against campus placement and internship job descriptions to see which skills and keywords recruiters expect, then add projects or certifications that cover the gaps.

Which job portals does it work with?

Any of them. Copy the job description from Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed, Foundit, Instahyre or a company careers page and paste it into ProfileIQ.

What file formats can I upload?

You can upload your resume as a PDF or Word (DOCX) file. Pro users can download the optimised resume in both PDF and Word formats.

Is my resume data safe?

Your resume is used only to generate your analysis. ProfileIQ does not sell your data. See the privacy policy for details on how data is stored and processed.
