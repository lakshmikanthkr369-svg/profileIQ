Why we built it

Your experience is real. Your resume should show it.

Many employers use applicant tracking systems (ATS) and keyword screening to decide which resumes a person actually reads. A strong candidate can be passed over simply because their resume doesn't use the words the job description does.

And if you're a fresher, you may not have a resume at all. Starting from a blank page, without knowing what recruiters look for, is a hard first step.

ProfileIQ shows you exactly what's missing and helps you fix it, so your resume says what you've actually done, in the language the role is asking for.
